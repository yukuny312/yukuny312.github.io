About me

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Johns Hopkins University, advised by Dr. Craig Jones and Dr. Gregory D. Hager.

My current sits at the intersection of medical imaging and artificial intelligence developing data-driven methods that aim to translate into real clinical impact. My research focuses on deep learning approaches for image-based diagnosis, disease progression modeling, and clinical decision support, with particular attention to robustness and practical usability. I integrate longitudinal imaging data (e.g., MRI and EUS) to capture disease evolution and predict patient-specific outcomes, especially in pancreatic cancer. In addition, I design hierarchical models to learn both global and localized imaging patterns and investigate radiomic texture features to improve stability and reproducibility..

Before my PhD, I received my M.S. in Computer Science from Johns Hopkins University.
